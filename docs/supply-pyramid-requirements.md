# Supply pyramid requirements

## Purpose

The frontline bunker pyramid should help a player decide what to deliver next. It is a priority guide, not a complete catalogue of every item in Foxhole. Quantities remain to be researched and agreed separately; do not infer target crate counts from these requirements.

## Coverage

The frontline profile must be reviewed across these categories:

- Base sustainment: Basic Materials for repair/building and Soldier Supplies for respawns.
- Infantry arms and matching ammunition, including basic rifles and relevant faction variants.
- Anti-tank weapons and ammunition, plus situational anti-structure equipment.
- Medical supplies: bandages, first aid and trauma kits, blood plasma, and medic equipment where appropriate.
- Defensive and field utility: grenades, smoke, shovels, radios, binoculars, gas protection, and fortification materials where useful.
- Specialist support: mortars and their ammunition, or other operation-specific supplies when the base is expected to use them.
- Base maintenance supplies only when required by current game mechanics and the base's maintenance role.

This is a review checklist, not a requirement to include every item in every preset. Faction availability, war progression, crate logistics, vehicle capacity, base role, existing stock, and current threats determine whether an item belongs and its priority.

## Priority model

- Keep a stable core for base survival and basic combat readiness.
- Allow situational groups or profiles for threats and roles such as armor pressure, infantry defense, artillery, construction/repair, and specialist operations.
- Use current stock and a target level to show deficits; do not recommend deliveries for items already at target.
- Make priority and target quantities editable per clan configuration. Values should be tuned after item and game-version validation.
- Avoid overstock recommendations for vulnerable forward bases.

## Current-game accuracy

Do not include the removed Bunker Supplies item. Maintenance Supplies are the current renamed/replacement supply for decay prevention. Verify item existence and faction availability against the project catalog before adding codes.

## Research references

- Official Foxhole Wiki, [Logistics Quickstart](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Community_Guides/Logistics_Quickstart): frontline needs depend on nearby infrastructure; Basic Materials, Soldier Supplies, anti-tank weaponry, rifles, and ammunition are described as persistent needs. It also warns against oversupplying vulnerable frontline bases.
- Official Foxhole Wiki, [Medical items](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Medical) and [Medic Guide](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Community_Guides/Medic_Guide): categories and roles of medical supplies.
- Official Foxhole Wiki, [Bunker Base](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Bunkerbase): respawn, stockpile, garrison, and maintenance roles.
- Official Foxhole Wiki, [Bunker Supplies](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Bunker_Supplies): documents removal in update 1.52 and replacement by Maintenance Supplies; treat this page as historical context only.
- Community, [FMAT frontline supply pyramid](https://www.reddit.com/r/foxholegame/comments/1hinrnv/warden_logistics_guide_fmat_frontline_supply/): useful layered-priority example, but version-specific and explicitly situational.
- Community tool, [Foxlogi bunker supply guide](https://foxlogi.com/guide/06-bunker-supply/): example of target stock by base tier, adjustable multiplier, stock deficit calculation, and truck-capacity-aware delivery suggestions.
